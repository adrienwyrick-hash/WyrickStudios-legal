# Privacy Policy for WS Drift

_Last updated: May 29, 2026_

Wyrick Studios built the WS Drift app as a free game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use WS Drift.

## Information We Collect

WS Drift does not require an account. The app integrates these services that may collect data:

### Google AdMob
WS Drift displays ads via Google AdMob, including rewarded ads for the revive feature. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (with your permission), device information, ad interaction data, and approximate location from IP address. A consent prompt appears on first launch where required.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Sign in with Apple (Optional)
If you choose to Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and email (or a private relay email) with WS Drift for identity display. You can revoke this in iOS Settings.

### Apple Game Center
WS Drift submits scores to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center stores your player ID and scores per Apple's privacy practices.

Apple's policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Data Stored On Your Device
Your best score and sound/music preferences are stored locally and are removed if you uninstall the app.

## Children's Privacy
WS Drift is not directed to children under 13.

## Your Rights
You can reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings, revoke Sign in with Apple, or delete the app to remove local data.
