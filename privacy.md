# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: June 6, 2026**

Wyrick Studios is the publisher of the games listed below. This policy describes what data each app handles and how. Each section is specific to one app — different products may collect or store different things.

---

## WS Drift

WS Drift is an arcade game developed by Adrien Wyrick.

### Information We Collect

WS Drift itself does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. All gameplay data stays on your device unless you choose to use the optional Apple services described below.

### Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. None of it is sent to the developer.

- Best score and per-run statistics (ships destroyed, bosses defeated, obstacles cleared, etc.)
- Sound, music, and haptics preferences
- Selected fighter ship
- Achievement progress and daily streak counter
- Onboarding state
- Sign in with Apple credentials, if you choose to sign in (locally cached identity tokens — see Apple Services below)

### Apple Services

WS Drift integrates with the following Apple services. Each is subject to Apple's own privacy policy (<https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>).

- **Game Center**: When you tap Game Center inside the app, your score and achievement progress are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Sign-in is handled by your device's Apple ID. You may opt out at any time via Settings → Game Center.
- **Sign in with Apple**: Optional. If you choose to sign in, Apple shares your name and email (or a private relay email if you choose to hide it). WS Drift stores only the identifier and display name locally. You can revoke access at any time via Settings → Apple ID → Sign In with Apple.
- **In-App Purchases**: Premium fighter ships are sold through the App Store as Non-Consumable products. Apple processes the transaction; WS Drift only receives the resulting entitlement status from the StoreKit framework. No payment information is transmitted to or stored by WS Drift.

### Third-Party Services

WS Drift does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

### Sharing Your Score

The Share Score button on the Game Over screen generates an image on your device and hands it to Apple's system share sheet. WS Drift does not receive information about what you share or where you share it. Any service you share through (Instagram, Messages, Twitter, etc.) handles the data per its own privacy policy.

### Children's Privacy

WS Drift does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional Apple services above may have their own age and consent requirements — refer to Apple's privacy policy for details.

### Deleting Your Data

You can clear all locally stored WS Drift data at any time via **Options → Privacy → Delete My Data**. This removes your best score, achievement progress, daily streak, ship selection, sign-in identity, and preferences from your device. Game Center scores and Sign in with Apple are managed by Apple — use iOS Settings to remove those separately.
