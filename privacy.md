# Privacy Policy for WS Drift

_Last updated: May 29, 2026_

Wyrick Studios ("we", "us", "our") built the WS Drift app as a free game.
This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, used, and shared
when you use WS Drift.

## Information We Collect

WS Drift itself does not require you to create an account. However, the app
integrates the following services that may collect data:

### Google AdMob (Advertising)
WS Drift displays ads served by Google AdMob, including rewarded ads used
for the in-game revive feature. AdMob may collect:
- Advertising identifier (IDFA, with your permission via Apple's App
  Tracking Transparency prompt)
- Device information (model, OS version, language, region)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)
- Approximate location derived from IP address

On first launch, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form may
appear in regions where it is required (e.g. EEA, UK). You can manage your
ad personalization choices through that prompt.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Sign in with Apple (Optional)
If you choose to Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and email
address (or a private relay email, at your choice) with WS Drift. This is
used only to display your identity within the app. You can revoke this at
any time in your Apple ID settings.

### Apple Game Center
WS Drift submits your high scores to Apple Game Center leaderboards. Game
Center stores your player ID, scores, and achievements according to Apple's
own privacy practices.

Learn more: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Information Stored on Your Device
Your best score and your sound/music preferences are stored locally on your
device using Apple's standard user defaults. This data never leaves your
device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

## Deleting Your Data

WS Drift includes a built-in data deletion control. Open the app, tap
**Options** on the home screen, then tap **Delete My Data**. Confirming
removes the following from your device:
- Your best score
- Your stored Sign in with Apple identity (name, email)
- Your sound and music preferences

Some data lives outside the app and cannot be deleted from within WS Drift:
- **Game Center scores** — managed by Apple. Remove them in iOS Settings →
  Game Center, or by contacting Apple Support.
- **Sign in with Apple authorization** — revoke in iOS Settings → Apple ID →
  Sign In with Apple → WS Drift → Stop Using Apple ID.
- **Google AdMob attribution data** — managed by Google. See Google's
  privacy controls at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Deleting the app from your device also removes all locally stored data.

## Children's Privacy
WS Drift is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
information, please contact us so we can remove it.

## Data Retention
We do not operate our own servers and do not store personal information
ourselves. Data collected by Google AdMob, Sign in with Apple, and Game
Center is retained according to those providers' own policies.

## Your Rights
You can:
- Disable personalized ads through the consent prompt or in iOS Settings
- Revoke Sign in with Apple in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Sign In with Apple
- Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Tracking
- Delete the app to remove all locally stored data

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted
on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
