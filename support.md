## Common questions

### How do I delete my account/data?
Open WS Drift → **Main Menu** → **Options** → **Privacy** section → tap **DELETE ACCOUNT**.

A single tap permanently removes your sign-in identity, best score, daily streak, achievement progress, and all preferences stored on the device. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

### How do I restore my purchases on a new device?
1. Install WS Drift on the new device
2. Open the app → **Main Menu** → **Hangar (Ship Select)**
3. Tap the **Restore** button at the bottom of the screen
4. Sign in to the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase

### Remove Ads isn't disabling ads
- Confirm the purchase appears in **Settings → [Your Name] → Media & Purchases → Subscriptions / Purchase History**
- In WS Drift, open the **Hangar** and tap **Restore**
- Force quit and relaunch the app
- If ads still appear, email us with your Apple ID purchase receipt

### Sign in with Apple
Sign in with Apple is optional. WS Drift uses it only to label your local profile on the device — we do not store your data on a server. You can hide your real email by selecting "Hide My Email" during the Apple Sign In flow.

### Privacy

### Game Center / Leaderboard
WS Drift uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is operated by Apple, not by Wyrick Studios. To manage your Game Center profile, go to **Settings → Game Center** on your device.

### Refunds
Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at:
https://reportaproblem.apple.com
